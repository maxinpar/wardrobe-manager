# A. BUY — ranked by fits unlocked

### A1 · Charcoal / mid-grey trousers — **the highest-value purchase in the wardrobe**
**Missing entirely.** There is no grey bottom of any kind. Dark bottoms are exactly two: the navy
wool and the black coated jean.

**Six shirts name it explicitly in their own catalogue cards** — `tops_18`, `tops_22`, `tops_23`,
`tops_25`, `tops_26`, `tops_27` all read *"grey trousers when those arrive"*. That phrase has been
sitting in the data since 27 August. It is the single most-requested item the catalogue contains.

It also fixes a structural rule problem: **a pale top needs a dark bottom**, and 4 of 9 trousers are
pale stone/sand. Grey is the neutral that takes every shirt colour you own — pink, lilac, blue
stripe, charcoal, black — where beige fights half of them.

- **Buy two, not one:** a wool-blend flat-front in charcoal (smart register, works with the grey
  blazer and the navy blazer), and a cotton/twill in mid-grey (everyday, sneaker-compatible).
- Spec: flat front, no pleats, mid-rise, slight taper, **no cuff**. W33–34 / EU44. Plain weave —
  avoid anything with a sheen, which reads as orphaned suit trouser.
- Sydney: [M.J. Bale](https://www.mjbale.com/collections/mens-sale-trousers-pants) (Sydney stores +
  online, the wool option) · [Politix](https://www.politix.com.au/) · Country Road ·
  Uniqlo AU smart ankle pants for the cheap cotton pair.
- Est. AUD 90–250 depending on cloth.

**Unlocks:** realistically 8–12 new fits immediately, because it pairs with shirts that currently
have only stone or indigo underneath them.

- **Brown/tobacco chino is absent.** Bottoms are pale, blue or black; nothing warm-mid.
  A tobacco or olive chino would give the navy and burgundy knits somewhere new to go.
- Pale blue is the most crowded colour you own — `tops_15`, `tops_25`, `tops_26`, `tops_27`,
  `tops_46`, `tops_47`. **Do not buy another light blue shirt.**
- Brown/tan leather is over-covered (four casual belts); black leather is thin at the smart end.

**Fabric**
- Two linen shirts, **no linen or summer-weight trouser** — a Sydney summer gap.
- Knitwear skews to V-necks; crews are thin and two of them are Replace.
- Nothing polyester should re-enter: `tops_38` was binned specifically because polyester
  short-sleeve doesn't breathe in a Sydney summer.

**Size — buy to these, the bin pile proves it**
- Shirts: **neck 16" / 41cm** (`tops_29` binned at 15in — it doesn't close), **sleeve 34–35"**
  (two shirts binned for sleeves too long), **slim body, not extra-slim** (three binned for loose
  bodies; nothing was ever binned for being too tight).
- Trousers: **W33–34 / EU44**, and budget a hem on every pair — it has been the recurring flaw.
- Tops M; some Asian-sizing XXL equals M. Shoes EU44 / UK10 / US10.5. Belts 34–36in.
