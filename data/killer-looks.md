## Rules verified across all seven
- Belt colour tracks shoe colour in every fit (black/black x2, tan/chestnut x3, tan/tan x1, brown/brown x1).
- No pale-on-pale: every pale top sits on a dark bottom.
- No V-neck knit worn alone (tee gap still open).
- No `Bin` / `Replace` / `out`-of-scope items used.
- No navy-on-navy; the André brogue and the Ecco sneaker are both kept off the navy wool trouser.

## What's deliberately absent
None of the seven uses a V-neck, and there are **nine** in the wardrobe. All need a plain tee
underneath and none is on file. Two crew tees (white + grey, ~$20) unlock the Celio red cashmere,
Zara brown cashmere, raspberry, Banana Republic slate and Amazon grey simultaneously. Still the
highest-leverage purchase in the wardrobe.
