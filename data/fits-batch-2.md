# OFFICE — WARM WEATHER (6)

## W1. White polo & sage
**temp:** mild, warm · **rain_safe:** false (suede) · **formality:** 3
**good_for:** work, casual · **bad_for:** gym, formal

`tops_09_white-pique-polo` · `trousers_07_oxford-sage` · `shoes_08a_suede-penny-loafer` · `belts_03_oxford-arlen-tan`

**commentary:** The cleanest warm-weather answer you own. Sage is a muted mid-tone, so white on top
still has somewhere to sit — it reads crisp rather than washed out. Brown suede keeps it soft.
**catch:** No-show socks, and keep the loafers out of the wet — suede.

## W2. Grey V-neck & cobalt
**temp:** mild, warm · **rain_safe:** false (nubuck) · **formality:** 2
**good_for:** work, casual, weekend · **bad_for:** client, formal

`amazon-essentials-grey-vneck` + `tees_01_white-crew` · `trousers_03_decathlon-cobalt` · `shoes_03_ecco-black-nubuck` · `belts_11_black-classic-pin-buckle`

**commentary:** The cobalt chino has been sitting unused because nothing obvious went with it. Grey
is the answer — it's the one neutral that doesn't fight a saturated blue. Black sneaker and belt stop
it tipping into holiday-wear.
**catch:** White tee, not grey — a grey tee under a grey V-neck reads like you got dressed in the dark.

## W3. Blue V-neck & beige
**temp:** mild, warm · **rain_safe:** true · **formality:** 3
**good_for:** work, casual, client · **bad_for:** gym

`tissaia-blue-vneck` + `tees_01_white-crew` · `trousers_01_decathlon-beige` · `shoes_02_andre-tan-brogue` · `belts_03_oxford-arlen-tan`

**commentary:** Beige is your best-fitting chino and it's been underused. Mid-blue over it is the
most conventional good-looking thing in this list, which is the point — not every fit needs an idea.
**catch:** The brogue's blue sole is what stops this reading country-English. Don't swap it for the
Church's, which would make the whole thing too formal for the weather.

## W4. Brioni & Air Max
**temp:** mild, warm · **rain_safe:** false (mesh/suede) · **formality:** 3
**good_for:** work, casual, weekend · **bad_for:** client, formal

`tops_02_brioni-white-blue-collar-polo` · `trousers_09_celio-indigo-jeans` · `shoes_09b_nike-airmax-1` · `belts_04_distressed-brown-everyday`

**commentary:** Your best polo with sneakers and denim. High-low done deliberately — the Brioni
collar carries the whole fit, and the trainers stop it looking like you tried. This is the Friday
version of a smart polo.
**catch:** Whiten-wash the Brioni first; it's gone slightly creamy. Clean the Air Max — the toes are
grubby.

## W5. Brown cashmere & stone gingham
**temp:** mild, warm · **rain_safe:** true · **formality:** 3
**good_for:** work, client, dinner · **bad_for:** gym, golf

`zara-brown-cashmere-vneck` + `tees_01_white-crew` · `trousers_06_stone-gingham` · `shoes_04_churchs-apron-derby` · `belts_02_tan-vera-pelle`

**commentary:** Brown over pale stone with chestnut on the feet is a tonal fit that doesn't look like
you were trying to make one. The gingham waistband facing is a private detail — nobody sees it, you
know it's there.
**catch:** Brown-on-brown needs the tones to differ. The V-neck is warm mid-brown, the Church's are
chestnut — that gap is what makes it work. Don't add the brown Chelsea instead.

## W6. Red V-neck & stone
**temp:** mild, warm · **rain_safe:** true · **formality:** 2
**good_for:** work, casual, weekend · **bad_for:** client, formal

`zara-red-vneck` + `tees_01_white-crew` · `trousers_04_oxford-stone` · `shoes_07_oxford-brown-chelsea` · `belts_04_distressed-brown-everyday`

**commentary:** You own three reds and wear none of them. Red over pale stone is the easiest way in —
the trouser is quiet enough that the colour does the work without shouting.
**catch:** White tee only. A red tee under a red V-neck flattens both.

Capped at 2 on purpose. You own one blazer, one wool trouser and one genuinely smart shoe, so a third

genuinely different.

## S2. Overcoat over the shawl

**catch:** Spot-clean the mark near the left hip of the overcoat. Brown leather only with navy wool.

## Rules verified across all 20

- Belt colour tracks shoe colour in every fit — black/black ×9, tan/chestnut ×5, tan/brown-suede ×2,
  brown/brown ×4.
- No pale top over a pale bottom. Every pale top (white polo, pale-blue polo, Fedeli, Brioni) sits on
  a mid or dark bottom.
- Every V-neck has a tee under it. Eight fits use one; that was impossible before today.
- No navy-on-navy. The two closest calls (K2 navy over indigo, C3 slate vest over blue V-neck) are
  flagged in their own catches with a specific swap.
- No `Bin`, `Replace` or `out`-of-scope items. `shoes_01_black-chelsea` (Replace),
  `shoes_06_zegna-black-monk`, `shoes_08c_megis-driving-moc`, `shoes_09a_nike-airmax-running`,
  `tops_06_vuori-grey-green-polo`, `belts_08_cuater-grey-braided-stretch` and the pleather moto are
  all absent.
- The André brogue and the Ecco sneaker stay off `trousers_08_tyrwhitt-navy-wool`.
- `zara-black-rollneck` is absent — Max dislikes roll-necks.
