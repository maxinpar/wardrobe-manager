{#- The app's weather glyphs, defined once. Before this the weather was text everywhere — two chips in the header reading "24°C · warm" and "Dry". This Week needs a sun and a rain cloud at four sizes, and the rule from the handoff is that they get added centrally and the header chips start using them too, rather than the app growing a second weather vocabulary that happens to live on one screen. Stroke, not fill, at a 24px viewBox: they sit next to 13–20px type and a filled glyph at that size reads as a blob. `currentColor` is deliberately NOT used — a sun is amber and rain is slate wherever they appear, and inheriting the ink of whatever line they land in would make them grey. -#} {% macro sun(size=20, stroke=1.6) -%} {%- endmacro %} {% macro rain(size=20, stroke=1.6) -%} {%- endmacro %} {#- The one callers actually want: wet or dry, decided here so no template has to remember which way round the test goes. -#} {% macro glyph(wet, size=20, stroke=1.6) -%} {% if wet %}{{ rain(size, stroke) }}{% else %}{{ sun(size, stroke) }}{% endif %} {%- endmacro %}
